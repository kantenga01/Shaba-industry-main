# SHABA Industry — Corporate Website Specifications

## 1. Company Overview

**SHABA INDUSTRY** is a multi-service company based in Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC). The company specializes in industrial services, construction, safety, printing, logistics, industrial supplies, and IT services.

The corporate website must project a professional, modern, high-end, and industrial brand image to enhance credibility and generate new commercial leads.

## 2. Key Objectives

The primary goals of the website are to:

- Present the company and all its business divisions.
- Generate qualified quote requests (_Demandes de devis_).
- Facilitate fast direct communication with clients.
- Showcase completed projects and portfolio items.
- Highlight the diversity and expertise of SHABA INDUSTRY’s business units.
- Improve organic search visibility (SEO).

## 3. Brand Identity & Design Guidelines

- **Color Palette:** Yellow, Black, White.
- **Visual Style:** Modern, industrial, sleek/elegant, featuring subtle micro-animations.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully responsive across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices.

## 4. Website Architecture

### 4.1 Home Page

- **Hero Section:** Large dynamic slider featuring:
  - Headline: _"Your Trusted Industrial Partner"_
  - Call-to-Action (CTA) Buttons: `Request a Quote` | `Contact Us`
- Company Overview (Brief Introduction)
- Key Business Units & Services Summary
- "Why Choose SHABA INDUSTRY" Section
- Key Statistics & Performance Metrics
- Official Partners & Client Testimonials
- Comprehensive Footer

### 4.2 About Us Page

- Company Overview
- Vision, Mission, and Core Values
- Company History & Milestones
- Executive & Operational Leadership Team

## 5. Services & Business Units

Each service division will have a dedicated, detailed landing page.

### 5.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

- **Product Catalog:** Helmets, Work Gloves, Safety Goggles, Safety Footwear/Boots, Overalls/Coveralls, High-Visibility Vests, Respirator Masks, Safety Harnesses, and Protective Accessories.
- **Call to Action:** `Request a Quote` button.

### 5.2 SHABA Construction

- **Core Services:** Structural Construction, Industrial Plumbing, Facility Renovation, Industrial Civil Works, Building Maintenance.
- Integrated Project Portfolio / Photo Gallery.

### 5.3 SHABA Print

- **Services:** Digital Printing, Large-Format Printing, Flyers, Business Cards, Roll-up Banners, Kakemonos, Vinyl Banners, Vehicle Branding, Corporate Visual Communication.
- Integrated Portfolio Gallery.

### 5.4 Industrial & Mining Supplies

- **Product Catalog:** Mechanical Hand & Power Tools, Industrial Machinery/Equipment, Mining Supplies, Technical Consumables.
- downloadable PDF Product Catalog.

### 5.5 Import & Export

- **Services:** Logistics Management, Freight Transport, Import/Export Operations, Technical Equipment Procurement.
- Step-by-Step Logistics & Sourcing Process Overview.

### 5.6 IT Services & Digital Solutions

- **Offerings:** Custom Software Engineering, Web & Mobile Application Development, Cybersecurity, Digital Marketing, Enterprise Network Architecture, IT Systems Maintenance, Cloud Hosting, ERP Solutions.
- Detailed Technology Solutions Portfolio.

## 6. Portfolio / Projects

- Filterable Photo and Video Galleries.
- **Categories:** Construction, Industrial, Printing, IT Solutions, Logistics.

## 7. Resources & Catalogues

- Downloadable Corporate & Product Catalogs (PDF).
- Technical Specifications & Datasheets.
- Product Documentation.

## 8. Request a Quote Form

Interactive form featuring:

- Full Name, Company Name, Phone Number, Email Address.
- Target Service/Product Selection dropdown.
- Project Details / Scope Description field.
- File Upload attachment support (e.g., specs, RFPs).
- **Automated Delivery:** Form submissions automatically trigger formatted email notifications to the sales team.

## 10. Blog & Insights

- Articles & Thought Leadership covering Construction, Industrial Trends, Safety/PPE Best Practices, Cybersecurity, Commercial Printing, and Technical Guides.

## 11. Careers

- Current Job Openings and Vacancies.
- Direct CV / Resume Submission Form.

## 12. Administration & Content Management System (CMS)

A secure admin dashboard to manage:

- Pages and Services content
- Portfolio entries and media
- Downloadable catalogs and documentation
- Blog articles and Job listings
- Quote requests and incoming contact form submissions
- User accounts and permissions

## 13. Technical Features & Functionality

- **Multilingual Support:** French / English
- **Fully Responsive Design** & Mobile Optimization
- **SEO Optimization** (Meta tags, structured data, clean URLs)
- **SSL Security Certificate Integration**
- **Third-Party Integrations:** WhatsApp Direct Chat, Google Maps, Social Media Links, Google Analytics tracking
- **Interactive Modules:** Search functionality, Quote Form with file upload support, Newsletter subscription

## 14. Technical Stack Recommendations

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS / Bootstrap (Optional: React / Vue.js)
- **Backend:** Laravel (v12 / v13)
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Hosting & Infrastructure:** Linux VPS running Nginx or Apache

## 15. Expected Performance Standards

- **Page Load Speed:** Under 3 seconds
- **Cross-Browser Compatibility:** Full support for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari
- **Security Standards:** CSRF protection, XSS prevention, data sanitization, and automated regular backups

## 16. Project Deliverables

1. Complete, production-ready website.
2. Fully documented source code repository.
3. Configured production database.
4. Functional Administrative Dashboard.
5. User Guide and Technical Documentation.
6. Deployment and launch on company primary domain (`shabaindustry.com`).
